Fion credentials helper

Makes the salted hash for a username and password. Nothing is sent anywhere: it runs in your browser. Use it on your own computer, never on a shared one.

Customer sign-in: paste the result over CRED= in login.html.
Admin sign-in: replace the matching entry in the ADMINS= list in both manager-login.html and admin.html and keep its "role" ("super" or "support").
